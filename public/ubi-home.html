Why UBI Needs AI

The Problem

Manual aid distribution is slow, opaque, and expensive. 30% of aid funds are lost to overhead, administrative delays, and corruption. Citizens wait weeks for disbursements with no visibility into the process.

The Solution

Bridge AI OS automates eligibility verification, distribution, and tracking -- with 100% transparency on-chain. Every Rand is accounted for, every distribution is verifiable, and overhead drops to near zero.
