Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Updated: 2026-04-01

Uptime Commitment

Bridge AI OS targets 99.9% monthly uptime for Pro and Enterprise subscribers. Starter tier has no uptime guarantee.

Response Times

  • Critical (service down): 1 hour response, 4 hour resolution target
  • High (major feature impacted): 4 hour response, 24 hour resolution target
  • Medium (degraded performance): 8 hour response, 72 hour resolution target
  • Low (minor issue): 24 hour response, 5 business day resolution target

Credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, eligible subscribers receive service credits: 10% for 99.0-99.9%, 25% for 95.0-99.0%, 50% for below 95.0%.

Exclusions

SLA does not apply during scheduled maintenance (announced 48h in advance), force majeure events, or issues caused by customer infrastructure.
