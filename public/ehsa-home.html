The Healthcare Challenge

The Problem

Africa's healthcare system serves 1.4 billion people with only 3% of the world's health workers. Paper records, fragmented systems, and no digital infrastructure mean patients fall through the cracks every day.

The Solution

EHSA digitizes patient records, automates compliance, and enables AI-assisted diagnostics -- accessible from any device, in any clinic, across the continent.
