Data & Ethics

Built responsibly, with African healthcare at the center.

Trained on African Health Data

Healthcare AI trained predominantly on Western data fails African patients. EHSA Brain is built on diverse datasets reflecting the conditions, genetics, and environments of the African continent.

Privacy First

Full POPIA compliance. All patient data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Explicit patient consent required. Data residency in South Africa.

Human in the Loop

AI assists, doctors decide. Every AI recommendation is presented as a suggestion, never an instruction. Clinical judgment always has the final word.
