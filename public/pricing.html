Frequently asked questions
Can I switch plans at any time? ▼
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. When upgrading, you pay the prorated difference. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept Visa, Mastercard via PayFast (South Africa), Paystack (Africa), and cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT, BRDG). Annual plans via EFT available on request.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? ▼
The Starter tier gives permanent access to public features. Paid plans include a 14-day money-back guarantee — try risk-free.
What happens if I exceed my API limits? ▼
Requests beyond your daily limit are queued and throttled — not blocked. You'll receive an alert to upgrade. Enterprise and Platform tiers have burst capacity built in.
Do you offer annual discounts? ▼
Yes — toggle annual billing above to save 20% on all paid plans. Custom multi-year agreements available for Enterprise and Platform.
What is the white-label Platform option? ▼
Platform customers get a fully branded deployment of Bridge AI OS under their own domain with custom theming, dedicated infrastructure, their own user base, and a revenue-sharing model.

Need a custom solution?

Enterprise and Platform customers get dedicated onboarding, custom SLAs, and priority engineering access.
